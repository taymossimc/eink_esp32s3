# Flux schematic — SIM7600 M.2 modem + external SIM (replaces J6 dongle)

Module: DFRobot TEL0158 / SIM7600G-H-M2 (M.2 B-KEY, 42×31.4 mm)
NOT Mini-PCIe — use M.2 B-key WWAN socket (67-position card edge).
Datasheet: documentation/SIM7600/TEL0158_sim7600g-h-m2-4g-communication-module_datasheet_V1.0.pdf
SIMCom HW ref: SIM7600G_SIM7600G-H_Hardware Design V1.00

## Remove
- J6 (1×4 bench USB-dongle header) — delete or repurpose

## Add

### J9 — M.2 B-key socket (replace J6 function)
- Accepts TEL0158 module; antennas are on-module IPEX4 (layout: keep edge clearance, no traces)
- PERIPH_5V_SW feeds modem buck input (same switched rail as display)

### J10 — Nano SIM socket (6-pin push-push, e.g. Amphenol C707 10M006 512 class)
- C1 → MODEM_USIM_VDD
- C2 → MODEM_USIM_RST
- C3 → MODEM_USIM_CLK
- C5 → GND
- C6 → NC
- C7 → MODEM_USIM_DATA
- ESD protection on all USIM lines per SIMCom §3.8

Wire J10 to J9 M.2 USIM pins (module pins 17–20 via M.2 mapping).

### U_modem — Buck regulator PERIPH_5V_SW (~5.3 V) → MODEM_VBAT 3.8 V
- VBAT range 3.4–4.2 V; target 3.8 V nominal
- Peak ≥ 2 A (GSM burst per SIMCom Table 6)
- Bulk: ≥470 µF low-ESR + 100 nF at J9 VBAT pins (SIMCom recommends 300 µF–1000 µF)

### U_level — UART level shifter 1.8 V (module) ↔ 3.3 V (ESP32)
- SIM7600 UART is 1.8 V (SIMCom §3.3); ESP32 GPIO10/11 are 3.3 V
- Use TXB0108 (2+ channels) or equivalent auto-directional shifter
- VCCA 1.8 V from module VDD_1V8 (M.2 pin 15) or local LDO from MODEM_VBAT
- VCCB 3.3 V from UPS_3V3

### UART (crossed, unchanged firmware nets)
- MODEM_TX (ESP GPIO10, J1:16) → level shifter B→A → module RXD (pad 68)
- MODEM_RX (ESP GPIO11, J1:17) ← level shifter A←B ← module TXD (pad 71)
- RTS/CTS/DTR: NC unless needed later

### Power control
- PWRKEY (pad 3): auto-power via 0 Ω to GND per SIMCom (or 100 nF + ESD to GND)
- RESET (pad 4): NC (internal 40 kΩ pull-up to 1.8 V)
- FLIGHTMODE (pad 54): NC or pull-up (normal mode)
- USIM_DET (pad 53): NC if socket has mechanical detect unused

### Ground
- Common GND: J9, J10, buck, level shifter, ESP

## Nets (keep existing names where possible)
- MODEM_TX, MODEM_RX → through U_level to J9
- PERIPH_5V_SW → U_modem VIN, J9 optional USB_VBUS NC
- MODEM_VBAT (new) → J9 VBAT pins 38,39,62,63
- MODEM_USIM_* (new) → J10 ↔ J9
- GND, UPS_3V3 unchanged

## Firmware (no change required)
- modem_config.h: MODEM_TX=10, MODEM_RX=11, 115200 8N1
- POWER_ENABLE_GPIO=4 switches PERIPH_5V_SW which feeds modem buck

## PCB layout constraints (after schematic ERC clean)
- **LOCKED:** H1–H4 (58×49 mm) and J8 relative positions — DO NOT move
- J8: bottom layer, Pin_1 lower-right top view (current rotation)
- J4 (NFC mikroBUS): place at **board edge** for tap access
- J1/J1B: preserve 25.40 mm row spacing; leave USB-C clearance on Freenove side
  (USB ports sit between J1 pin01=5V and J1B pin01=GND region)
- J9 M.2 socket: place for module insertion; keep IPEX antenna clearance at board edge
- J10 SIM: accessible edge, short traces to J9 USIM nets
