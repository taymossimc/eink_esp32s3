## Power topology (prototype)

- Waveshare UPS 3S (23884) regulated output feeds ESP32 DevKit 5V input (J1 pin 21).
- **Measured UPS output: 5.3 V** (label net UPS_5V but document nominal/measured 5.3 V in properties and power budget).
- UPS 3V3 aux rail (~3.3 V) feeds NFC always-on; exact measured value TBD.
- GPIO4 HIGH enables ONE external switched 5V rail.
- Switched 5V feeds: Pimoroni Inky 13.3" (PIM774) AND SIM7600G-H modem.
- NFC MIKROE-3971 on always-on 3.3V (UPS_3V3) for wake-on-tap while 5V rail is OFF.
- NO firmware-controlled separate 3.3V e-paper rail.
- Boot: GPIO4 LOW ~3s then HIGH (modem power cycle). Sleep: GPIO4 LOW cuts 5V rail.

## Connector updates

### J2 UPS power (unchanged)
- Pin1 UPS_5V, Pin2 UPS_3V3, Pin3 GND

### J3 UPS monitor (unchanged concept)
- Pin4 BAT_SCL, Pin8 BAT_SDA (to J1 pins 8 and 20)

### J4 NFC mikroBUS (unchanged)
- I2C pins 11/12, INT pin 15, 3V3 pin 7 from UPS_3V3

### J5 E-paper 1x12
- Pin2: switched 5V (PERIPH_5V_SW) — NOT separate 3V3 switch
- Remove EPD_3V3_SW net entirely
- SPI/control nets per GPIO table above

### J6 Modem 1x4
- Pin1: PERIPH_5V_SW (from switched 5.3 V UPS rail)
- Pin2: GND
- Pin3: MODEM_RX (modem TX -> ESP GPIO11)
- Pin4: MODEM_TX (ESP GPIO10 -> modem RX)
- **Modem UART logic: 3.3 V** — direct connect to ESP32 GPIO10/11, no level shifter on bench prototype

## Parts to remove/simplify

Current schematic has dual P-MOS Q1 (NTMD6P02R2G) splitting EPD_3V3_SW and PERIPH_5V_SW, plus Q2 for 3V3 gate.

Remove:
- EPD_3V3_SW net and J5 pin1 connection to it
- Q2, R1, R3, R5, C1, C2 (3V3 switch path)
- Dual-channel use of Q1 — replace with single P-MOS 5V high-side switch

Keep:
- Q3 (N-ch gate driver) + R4, R6, R2, C3, C4 for GPIO4 -> 5V P-MOS gate
- Or simplify to one P-MOS + one N-ch if cleaner

Update all Role/Details properties: "bench prototype wiring" not "provisional dual-rail".
